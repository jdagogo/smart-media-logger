## Series Overview

**Pluribus** (stylized PLUR1BUS) is an American post-apocalyptic science fiction series set primarily in Albuquerque, New Mexico. After an extraterrestrial virus transforms humanity into a peaceful hive-mind collective called "the Others," novelist **Carol Sturka** finds herself among only 12-13 people on Earth immune to "the Joining." The series explores her resistance, grief, and the philosophical tensions between individual autonomy and collective harmony.

#### Plot Summary
A global catastrophe unfolds as an extraterrestrial virus transforms humanity into a hive-mind consciousness. Scientists had replicated the virus after discovering it through a radio signal. Carol Sturka, a miserable fantasy romance novelist in Albuquerque, witnesses her partner **Helen** collapse during the initial transformation. At a hospital, the infected respond in unison: "We just want to help, Carol."

A government official, **Davis Taffler**, contacts Carol to explain she is one of only 12 unaffected humans on Earth. Her emotional outbursts cause the hive-mind to seize, reportedly killing 11 million people in the process.

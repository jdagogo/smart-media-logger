## Series Overview

**Pluribus** is an American post-apocalyptic science fiction drama series created by **Vince Gilligan**. Set primarily in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the series follows novelist **Carol Sturka** (Rhea Seehorn), one of approximately 12-13 humans on Earth who are immune to an extraterrestrial virus that has transformed humanity into a peaceful hive-mind collective called "the Others."

> *Source: [Esquire], [Autostraddle], [Apple TV+]*

### Logline
After an alien virus transforms humanity into a collective consciousness, a cynical romance novelist must decide whether to fight for individuality or surrender to a world without conflict.

> *Source: [Apple TV+]*

### Premise Details
- Scientists discovered the virus through a radio signal and replicated it
- The transformation is called "the Joining"
- Immune individuals number only 12-13 worldwide
- The hive-mind cannot lie but operates like an AI model
- Carol's emotional outbursts can affect the collective, reportedly killing 11 million people

> *Source: [Esquire Ep1], [Autostraddle Ep3]*

### Plot Summary

At a hospital, the infected respond in unison: "We just want to help, Carol."

A government official, **Davis Taffler**, contacts Carol to explain she is one of only 12 unaffected humans on Earth. Her emotional outbursts cause the hive-mind to seize, reportedly killing 11 million people in the process.
